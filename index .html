About us

The technical arm of UOKCSA

The ICT Department of the St Augustine Catholic Students’ Association looks after the association’s website, sound, media and digital records. We also offer these skills to the rest of the University of Kabianga community.

Our work is run by students, for students. We aim to keep every service affordable, reliable and delivered on time.

Student-runBuilt and maintained by members of the association.
Mobile-firstOur digital work is made for the phones most students use.
Open to everyoneIndividuals, groups and student businesses are welcome.
